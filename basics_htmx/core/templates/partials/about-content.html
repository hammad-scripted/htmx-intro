A LITTLE ABOUT US

Make space for good ideas.

Fieldnotes is a small, welcoming place to keep track of the products and ideas you’re bringing to life. This demo pairs Django’s dependable foundations with HTMX’s quick, focused page updates.

Add an idea in the workspace and it’s saved to the database. Browse the collection with “Show more”; each update happens in place, without a full page reload.
